FoodBuddy

Scan it. Understand it. Buy better.

FoodBuddy turns any packaged-food barcode into a straight answer: a health score you can check the workings of, and a Safe, Caution, or Avoid verdict judged against your own allergies and dietary rules.

Scores you can audit

The score is computed on your device from published, versioned rules — nutrient thresholds, a cited additive risk table, and processing level. The same product always scores the same. AI explains ingredients in plain English; it never decides your score or your verdict.
