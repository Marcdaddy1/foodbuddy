Delete your account

You can permanently delete your FoodBuddy account and everything stored with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open FoodBuddy and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account and data are removed immediately.

By email (if you no longer have the app)

Email {{support_email}} from the address you used to sign up, with the subject “Delete my FoodBuddy account”.

We verify the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. We will confirm by email once it is done.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent. It is not a deactivation, and the account cannot be recovered afterwards. We remove:

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your dietary profile — allergies, intolerances, diet patterns, and your custom avoid-list
  • Your scan history and the verdicts saved with it
  • Your favourites and any notes you wrote
  • Your shopping lists and their contents

What is kept

Anonymous product information — the food catalogue itself, such as barcodes, ingredients, and nutrition data — is not personal data and stays in our database. Nothing in it identifies you or links back to your account.

Want a copy first?

Before deleting, you can export everything on your account: Profile → Account → Export my data. That produces a JSON file with all of the above. Once the account is deleted we cannot recover it for you.

Privacy policy · FoodBuddy

FoodBuddy provides general information about packaged food. It is not medical advice. If you have a food allergy, always read the physical product label.
